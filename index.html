    How each check is scored

    1. Money handle (3): automatic once the SharpAPI key is in: DraftKings splits are pulled at 7am and about an hour before each kickoff window (Thu 6:15 pm, Sun 11:15 am, 2:15 pm and 6:15 pm, Mon 6:15 pm CT). Money % 10+ points above bet % on a side = 3 points to that side, from the spread, or the moneyline if the spread shows nothing; if they point different ways it's 0. Circa (a sharper book) is shown for comparison. Every read is graded after the game under Season results, so after a month you'll know if it earns its 3 points.
    2. Pass rush vs protection (2): each defense's sack and pressure rate against the other line's sack rate allowed. Uses the listed starting QB's rushing over this season and the two before it (this season counts 1.5x): mobile (20+ rush yards a game on designed runs and scrambles) halves the rush edge against him; a pocket passer takes it in full. Defense-vs-QB-type splits and alerts only use clear cases (23+ yards mobile, 17 or less pocket) and count points the defense allowed, without pick-sixes, fumble returns or kick/punt return TDs. A backup starter (not the opening-day starter and hasn't started 3 straight) adds to the opposing rush edge. Cards flag Top-10 rush defenses by pressure rate or sacks. Blitz splits aren't in the free data, so check PFF or Next Gen Stats on close calls.
    3. Run game (2): rushing YPC (two-thirds season, one-third last 3 games) above league average plus the opponent's YPC allowed above average. Edge must exceed 0.25 yards a carry.
    4. Turnovers (2): your takeaways/g plus their giveaways/g, minus the reverse. Edge must exceed 0.4 a game.
    5. Red zone defense (2): RZ trips allowed/g and RZ TD % allowed, both standardized. Linebacker health isn't scored; split 1–1 by hand if a starting LB is out.
    6. Situational spot (1): home team's home win % vs road team's road win % (15-point gap), and a 2+ day rest edge. If they disagree, rest wins.
    7. Media check (Check 9, totals): every morning and before each kickoff window the site reads NFL.com (which carries NFL Network's coverage) and ESPN articles on each game and tags over talk ("take the over 46.5", "shootout") and under talk ("under 46.5", "defensive battle"). If 3+ articles lean one way with 75%+ agreement, or 75%+ of DraftKings bets are on one side of the total, it suggests the other side. Every call is graded after the game.
    8. Line move (1): manual. Point to the side the line moved toward with no news; to the other side if the move followed one big result.

    Pass rules checked automatically: the side loses both trench checks; the side is an underdog ranked bottom 5 in TDs/game or RZ TD %. Still on you: the total moving 3+ from the open, QB status 90 minutes out, and betting the team instead of the number.
